MedFacts LinkedIn Recap

MedFacts 101 started as a weekly series of Instagram slideshows and blog posts. This page is a home for the LinkedIn side of that project — short recaps of each week's post, written for a slightly different audience: clinicians, public-health folks, and anyone who found MedFacts 101 through LinkedIn instead of Instagram.

Antimicrobial resistance is a subject that keeps coming up across the sources MedFacts 101 draws from — public health agencies like the WHO and CDC track it as an ongoing global concern, and it's part of why this project exists in the first place. Rather than restate specific figures here, each recap will link back to the full blog post and, where relevant, to the primary source it's based on, so you can read the original research or guidance yourself.

New recaps go up alongside each week's blog post. If there's a topic you'd like covered — on Instagram, the blog, or here — see the contact page for how to reach out.
